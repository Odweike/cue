# Cue 0.1.7

Sharper timeline hover previews that stay out of the way.

## Install

1. Download **Cue-0.1.7.dmg** below.
2. Open the disk image and drag **Cue** into **Applications**.
3. Open Cue from Applications.

Requires **macOS 26** or later.

If macOS says the app cannot be opened, Control-click Cue → **Open**.

## Highlights

- Timeline hover frames are upright and keep the video’s aspect ratio
- Preview sits above the playback bar, or below when there is no room at the top
- Thumbnails are larger and rendered at Retina resolution
